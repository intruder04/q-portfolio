How this is worked out

  • Value is each week's shares times that week's closing price, adjusted for splits; the last point uses today's prices. Cash in your account isn't included, only stocks.
  • Put in is what you spent on buys minus what you got from sales. Buys paid for with dividends count as money put in, and the dividends count as gain, so nothing is counted twice.
  • The same money in the market buys the index with each purchase's dollars on the same day and sells it with each sale's dollars. Index prices include reinvested dividends. This is the fairest "what if": the same amounts, at the same times.
  • Return (%) is time-weighted: it shows how your picks grew, ignoring when you added money, so it's the right number to compare with an index. Your yearly return is money-weighted: what your actual timing earned, per year.
  • Largest drop is the biggest fall from a previous high in the time-weighted return.
  • Prices come from Yahoo Finance. A stock that's no longer listed uses the prices you traded at.
